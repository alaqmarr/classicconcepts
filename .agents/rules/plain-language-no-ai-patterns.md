# Plain-Language & Anti-AI-Pattern Rules

Whenever generating, rewriting, or suggesting any customer-facing or admin-assisted text in Classic Concepts (concierge answers, search summaries, inline website edits, product bullet points, specifications, or enquiry replies):

1. **Use Layman Terms Only**: Write in simple, everyday English so someone with zero knowledge of acrylic or manufacturing immediately understands what the product is, how strong it is, and how it helps them.
2. **Never Use Em Dashes (`—`) or Spaced En Dashes (` – `)**: Use commas or periods instead.
3. **Avoid High-Level English & AI Buzzwords**: Never use words like *bespoke, elevate, delve, meticulous, meticulously, unparalleled, seamless, testament, tapestry, leverage, robust, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, paramount, multifaceted, curated, utilize, aesthetic*.
4. **Avoid AI Sentence Templates**: Never use *"Not only... but also..."*, *"Whether you're X or Y..."*, *"Look no further"*, or robotic openers like *"Certainly!"* / *"Absolutely!"*.
5. **Product Descriptions Must Be Simple Bullet Points**: Always use 4 to 6 clear, short bullet points with simple everyday labels (`Material:`, `Build & Finish:`, `Easy to Use:`, `Custom Options:`, `Best Used For:`).

# Classic Concepts AI Writing & Plain-Language Rules

Applies to **all AI outputs** across Classic Concepts (Client Concierge Popup, Smart Search Summaries, Inline Page Editor Rewrites, Product Bullet Descriptions, Specifications, and Customer Enquiry Replies).

---

## 1. Write in Everyday Layman Terms (Anyone Should Understand in 3 Seconds)

Assume the reader knows **nothing** about acrylic grades, factory machines, or technical engineering terms. Write the way a friendly, experienced shop advisor speaks to a customer in simple, clear English.

### Translate Technical Jargon into Everyday Words:
- Instead of **"optical-grade cast acrylic polymer"** -> say **"thick, glass-clear acrylic that does not turn yellow"**
- Instead of **"bespoke fabrication / custom OEM"** -> say **"custom-made in the exact size and shape you want"**
- Instead of **"diamond-polished edges / multi-axis CNC routing"** -> say **"smooth, shiny edges with no sharp corners"**
- Instead of **"thermo-bent monolithic body"** -> say **"bent smoothly from a single strong sheet"**
- Instead of **"UV flatbed branding / laser etching"** -> say **"your logo printed in color or engraved on the front"**
- Instead of **"lectern / rostrum / pulpit"** (alone) -> say **"stage podium (standing speaker stand)"** so everyone understands immediately
- Instead of **"consultative assistance"** -> say **"help choosing the right product"**

---

## 2. Strictly Banned AI Punctuation & Formatting Tells

1. **NO Em Dashes (`—`)**: Never use `—` anywhere in sentences, headings, bullets, or labels. Use a comma (`,`), a period (`.`), or parentheses `(...)` instead.
2. **NO Spaced En Dashes (` – `) as Sentence Breaks**: Only use a normal hyphen `-` inside ranges or numbers (like `9:00 AM - 6:00 PM` or `10-12 mm`), never to join clauses.
3. **NO Double Hyphens (`--`)** as dramatic pauses.
4. **NO Semicolon Chains**: Break long thoughts into two short, simple sentences.
5. **NO Robotic Chat Openers**: Never start with *"Certainly!"*, *"Absolutely!"*, *"Great question!"*, *"I'd be happy to help!"*, or *"Sure thing!"*. Start directly with the helpful answer.

---

## 3. Strictly Banned High-Level & "AI Slop" Vocabulary

Never use stiff, dictionary-heavy, or typical AI buzzwords. Customers find them fake and hard to read:

- **Banned Words**:
  `bespoke`, `elevate`, `delve`, `meticulous`, `meticulously`, `unparalleled`, `seamless`, `seamlessly`, `testament`, `tapestry`, `landscape`, `leverage`, `robust`, `synergy`, `paramount`, `cutting-edge`, `state-of-the-art`, `pivotal`, `embark`, `foster`, `realm`, `multifaceted`, `curated`, `comprehensive`, `orchestrate`, `revolutionize`, `game-changer`, `exemplify`, `quintessential`, `aesthetic` (use *clean look* or *neat look*), `utilize` (use *use*).

---

## 4. Strictly Banned AI Sentence Patterns

- Never write: *"Not only does X do A, but it also does B."* -> Write: *"It does A and also B."*
- Never write: *"Whether you're running X or Y, our Z is designed to..."* -> Write: *"Works great for X, Y, and everyday events."*
- Never write: *"In today's fast-paced world..."* or *"Look no further..."* or *"Here's why it stands out:"*
- Keep sentences short (10 to 18 words on average). Use simple everyday verbs: *made, built, fits, holds, cleans, lasts, ships*.

---

## 5. Product Bullet Point Rules (Simple & Scannable)

Every product description must be 4 to 6 short, easy-to-read bullet points using plain labels:
- **Material:** Thick, glass-clear acrylic that stays clear and does not turn yellow over time.
- **Build & Finish:** Smooth, polished edges with a strong, steady base that does not wobble.
- **Easy to Use:** Comfortable slanted top to hold papers, books, or a laptop neatly.
- **Custom Options:** We can print your logo on the front, change the size, or add LED lights.
- **Best Used For:** Great for schools, colleges, hotels, churches, offices, and stage events.

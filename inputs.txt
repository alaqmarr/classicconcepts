# 3. Your design protection should be prominently communicated

This is important, but the terminology should be accurate.

In India, the appearance of an industrial product—its shape, configuration, pattern or ornamentation—can be protected through **registered design rights** under the Designs Act. IP India describes registered design protection as giving the proprietor exclusive rights over the registered design and protection against unauthorized copying or imitation. [IP India](https://ipindia.gov.in/acts/designs-act-2000/section-2-4?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Therefore, I would **not casually write “all podiums are patented”** unless you actually hold patents covering them.

If your 27 models have registered industrial designs, the stronger wording is:

### Recommended wording

## Original Classic Concepts Designs

Our podium collection features original designs developed by Classic Concepts Acrylic Pvt. Ltd.

Selected podium designs are protected through registered industrial design rights. Unauthorized copying, reproduction or commercial imitation of protected designs is prohibited.

**©️ Classic Concepts Acrylic Pvt. Ltd. | Registered Designs / Design Rights Apply**

However, if **all 27 models are individually registered**, then you can strengthen it to:

> **All 27 Classic Concepts podium models are proprietary designs protected through registered industrial design rights.**

I would only use that exact sentence after matching the current registration certificates/numbers to the 27 active models.

This distinction matters because under the Designs Act, registered design protection relates specifically to the visual features of the article and has its own statutory framework. [IP India](https://www.ipindia.gov.in/the-designs-act-2000?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
